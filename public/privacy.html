Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

PinForge Publisher (“the Service”) is a publishing and scheduling tool operated by the owner of afustitched.com. The Service helps users connect authorized Pinterest accounts, import content, schedule Pins, and publish Pins through Pinterest’s official API.

Information we collect

The Service may process the following information:

  • Account information needed to identify a connected Pinterest account, such as Pinterest user ID and username.
  • OAuth authorization data, including access and refresh tokens supplied by Pinterest when you authorize the Service.
  • Content you import for publishing, such as Pin titles, descriptions, image URLs, destination URLs, board information, scheduled dates, and related metadata.
  • Basic technical information required to operate and secure the Service, including information provided through hosting or security infrastructure.

How we use information

We use this information only as necessary to:

  • authenticate and maintain authorized Pinterest connections;
  • retrieve the Pinterest account and board information needed for publishing;
  • store and manage imported Pins and their schedules;
  • publish Pins that you have selected and approved through the Service;
  • detect publishing errors and maintain the reliability and security of the Service; and
  • respond to support or privacy requests.

Pinterest data

Pinterest account access is provided through Pinterest OAuth. The Service does not ask for or store your Pinterest password. OAuth tokens are stored in encrypted form and are used only to maintain the authorized connection and perform actions permitted by the scopes you approved.

You can revoke the Service’s Pinterest authorization through Pinterest. Revoking authorization may prevent the Service from publishing until the account is connected again.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data with third-party services only when necessary to operate the Service. Pinterest receives the information required to perform the Pinterest actions you authorize. The Service is hosted and operated using Cloudflare infrastructure, which may process technical and application data as part of providing its services.

Data security

OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted before being stored by the Service. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored information and access credentials. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Data retention and deletion

We retain account connection data and imported publishing records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain publishing history, prevent errors, and meet legitimate operational requirements. You may request deletion of your stored account and publishing data by contacting the Service operator.

Children’s privacy

The Service is intended for business and general adult use and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Service or its data practices change. The updated version will be published at this URL with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the Service operator through the contact details provided by the owner of afustitched.com.
